GOSPEL ATLAS - HOW TO OPEN
Coder: David Lee


OPEN IT IN THREE STEPS

  1. Save this ZIP file to your computer - the Desktop or Documents folder
     is fine. Do not work inside the ZIP preview window, and do not try to
     open it from an email preview.

  2. Unzip it.
       Windows - right-click the ZIP, choose "Extract All", then "Extract".
       macOS   - double-click the ZIP.
       Linux   - right-click and choose "Extract Here".

  3. Open the unzipped folder and double-click

       1 START HERE - Open Gospel Atlas.html

     Your browser opens a short page with one button on it: "Open the
     Gospel Atlas". Click it and the app loads.

  You can also skip the button and double-click Gospel_Atlas_David_Lee.html
  directly. That is the app itself.


IF IT OPENS IN THE WRONG PROGRAM

  Right-click Gospel_Atlas_David_Lee.html, choose "Open with", and pick
  Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari.


WHAT YOU NEED

  A recent Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari on Windows, macOS or Linux, with
  JavaScript enabled. A desktop or laptop computer is the intended target.
  No installation, account, terminal or web server is required, and the app
  needs no internet connection. The app file is large, so allow a few
  seconds for it to appear.
